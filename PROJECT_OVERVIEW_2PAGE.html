🌿 Project Overview & Mission Core Identity

NutriSmart AI combines evidence-based nutritional science, AI biometric meal personalization, and visual portion cheat-sheets to make healthy eating effortless, sustainable, and enjoyable. Features full light/dark mode and mobile responsiveness.

1️⃣ Page 1: Home (Welcome & Visual Spotlight) Route: 'home'

The primary entry point that immediately sets the clean, health-focused tone of the brand.

Left Column (Value Proposition): Main title: "Personalized Nutrition for a Healthier, Balanced Life" with intro text, CTA buttons (Explore Recipes, AI Meal Planner), and 3 benefit checkmarks.
Right Column (Visual Salad Bowl): High-definition colorful salad bowl photo with floating ambient glow and interactive badges: "🥗 Fresh & Wholesome" & "⚡ Balanced Nutrition".
3 Feature Highlight Cards: Wholesome Recipes (nutrient-dense real food), Daily Nutrition Tips (science-backed guidance), and Personalized Wellness (mindful vitality).
Daily Eating Tip & Recipe Preview: Interactive wisdom card with copy/save action + preview cards of wholesome recipes with calories and prep times.
2️⃣ Page 2: Daily Tips (Nutritional Wisdom Archive) Route: 'tips'

Daily micro-habits grounded in metabolic science and clinical research to build sustainable vitality.

Interactive Real-Time Search: Search bar allowing instant filtering by keywords like protein, hydration, gut, breakfast, sleep.
Category Navigation Pills: Quick filters: Gut Microbiome & Immunity, Metabolism & Energy, Hydration, and Mindful Habits.
Actionable Tip Cards: Includes headline, explanation, Science Insight callout, and a clear Action Step to implement today.
Save & Share Controls: One-click clipboard copy and local browser storage to bookmark favorite daily habits.
3️⃣ Page 3: Healthy Recipes (Wholesome Recipe Hub) Route: 'recipes'

A rich catalog of whole-food recipes designed for balanced macros, great taste, and quick home prep.

Filter by Goal & Diet: Filter categories: High Protein, Vegetarian, Low Carb, Heart Healthy + tags for Gluten-Free, Keto, Vegan.
Nutritional Badges: Every recipe displays exact Calories (kcal), Protein (g), Carbs (g), Healthy Fats (g), Fiber (g), and Cook Time.
Recipe Detail Pop-up Modal: Clicking any card opens a clean modal with full ingredients checklist, step-by-step instructions, and chef tips.
AI Score Indicator: Wholesomeness rating (e.g., 98/100) based on real-food ingredient density and absence of ultra-processed additives.

4️⃣ Page 4: AI Meal Planner (Personalized Nutrition Engine) Route: 'planner'

Algorithmic meal schedule generator customized to user biometrics and lifestyle targets.

Biometric Profile Form: Captures age, gender, height, weight, activity level multiplier, and health goal (Weight Loss, Muscle Building, Maintenance).
Dietary & Allergy Filters: Select preferences: Mediterranean, High Protein, Keto, Vegetarian, or Dairy-Free.
Personalized Day & Weekly Plan: Calculates targeted calories and macros, then structures Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, and Snack recommendations.
Automated Grocery List: Aggregates ingredients across all generated meals into a printable, categorized weekly shopping checklist.
5️⃣ Page 5: Nutrition Tools (Calculators & Macro Distribution) Route: 'tools'

Fast, scientific calculation tools for personal body metrics and macronutrient balancing.

Interactive BMI Calculator: Calculates Body Mass Index with color-coded classification: Underweight, Normal (18.5–24.9), Overweight, Obese.
BMR & TDEE Target Estimator: Computes Basal Metabolic Rate and Total Daily Energy Expenditure based on physical activity multipliers.
4 Macro Distribution Boxes: Visual breakdown for Proteins (4 kcal/g), Carbohydrates (4 kcal/g), Healthy Fats (9 kcal/g), and Fiber.
Goal Presets: One-click macro recalibration for Fat Loss (high protein), Muscle Hypertrophy, or Longevity/Maintenance.
6️⃣ Page 6: Nutrition Guide (Balanced Diet & Knowledge Hub) Route: 'guide'

Comprehensive visual education hub highlighting balanced diet foundations and interactive charts.

Why a Balanced Diet Matters: 4 pillars: All-Day Energy (no crashes), Immune Defense, Gut & Serotonin, and Metabolic Longevity.
The Sustainable 80/20 Rule: 80% whole nutrient-dense foods (vegetables, proteins, whole grains), 20% culinary flexibility without guilt.
Search Bar & 10 Topic Cards: Real-time search + filters (Balanced Diet, Macros, Micros, Gut, Timing). Cards have summaries, 💡 Smart Rules, & sources.
50-25-25 Smart Plate Method: Interactive plate diagram: 50% Veggies & Fruits, 25% Lean Protein, 25% Complex Carbs, plus hand portion rules.
Food Label Inspector (5/20 Rule): Clickable pins decoding serving sizes, calories per serving, hidden sugars, and the 5% (Low) / 20% (High) DV metric.
Eating Day Timeline: Chronobiological guide from 7:30 AM morning protein pacing to 7:30 PM screen-free mindful dinner.
🎤 3-Point Presentation Script (What to Say to Evaluators)
1. The Hook: "NutriSmart AI solves diet confusion by pairing biometric AI planning with simple visual rules like the 50-25-25 plate and 80/20 balance."
2. Technical Flow: "Built using React 19 and Vite with modular components, localStorage state persistence, instant search, and dynamic responsive CSS."
3. Key Impact: "Empowers users across 6 structured pages: from daily tips and macro calculation to personalized meal plans and label literacy."
